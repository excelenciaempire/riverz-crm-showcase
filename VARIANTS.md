# Riverz CRM product family

The ecommerce product and its sales adaptations share the [wacrm](https://github.com/ArnasDon/wacrm) foundation and Riverz extensions. The main commerce case covers merchant products, offers, delivery and customer support. The variants below adapt that same foundation to other business contexts.

## Riverz Dealers

An automotive-sales adaptation with vehicle inventory, buyer opportunities, visits, test drives and follow-up inside the inbox. My work adapts the product model and conversation flow to vehicles and salespeople, including Spanish/English views.

## Riverz Ventas

An internal SaaS-sales adaptation for qualification, nurturing, appointment setting and operations handoff. My work separates sales records from fulfillment: a won deal starts an operations record rather than automatically activating a merchant account.

## Shared implementation

TypeScript, React and Next.js, using the CRM/wacrm foundation. Reusable conversation behavior stays separate from business-specific catalogs, prices, identities and credentials. Source repositories remain private; this case makes no measured-sales claim.
