# CRM adaptations

These share the [wacrm](https://github.com/ArnasDon/wacrm) foundation and Riverz extensions. They are domain adaptations, not three independently built CRM engines.

| Workstream | Business context | Adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Riverz CRM | Ecommerce | Merchant products, offers, delivery and customer support |
| Riverz Dealers | Automotive sales | Vehicle context, buyer opportunities, visits and test drives |
| Riverz Ventas | SaaS sales | Qualification, nurturing, appointment setting and an internal operations handoff |

Reusable conversation behavior stays separate from business-specific catalogs, prices, identities and credentials. The repositories are private; this page summarizes the development work rather than claiming measured sales results.
